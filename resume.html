Summary

I am a QA leader with more than 10 years at BuzzFeed and HuffPost, helping teams ship web and mobile products with confidence. I grew QA from two engineers into a group of up to 8 direct reports, helped set our release standards, and managed a contractor program of up to 40 engineers, designers, and QA professionals across teams. Most recently I led the thinking behind two AI-assisted QA tools my team built, and I am preparing for the PMP exam.

Experience

Senior Engineering Manager, Quality Assurance

BuzzFeed, Inc. and HuffPost, New York, NY · April 2014 to August 2026

I owned the QA roadmap for BuzzFeed.com, HuffPost.com, and a growing mobile portfolio of up to 8 apps at a time, both live in the App Store and in continued development, across iOS and Android.

  • Managed a contractor program that went well beyond QA: up to 40 engineers, designers, and QA professionals across teams, with a multi-million dollar annual budget. Tracked headcount and allocation, planned capacity against the roadmap, and worked with Legal and Finance on SOWs and invoices.
  • Grew QA from two engineers into a multi-team group, with up to 8 direct reports including employees and contractors, and supported the team through a lot of organizational change. Held regular 1-on-1s and created a separate performance review process for contract engineers.
  • Moved QA from a final gate to an advocate for quality earlier in the process, joining roadmap planning so product and engineering knew what needed coverage. Fewer bugs reached late-stage QA, releases shipped faster, and quality metrics improved.
  • Started the team on a simple checklist-based manual process so everyone worked the same way, then led our move into test automation across mobile and web, using Bitrise for builds and the LambdaTest device cloud for testing.
  • Managed our QA tools and vendors. Saved more than $130,000 by ending an outside testing contract with little effect on coverage, renegotiated LambdaTest to cut costs by 25%, and avoided a price increase from a third-party vendor by finding build overages and changing our pipelines.
  • Led the thinking behind two AI-assisted QA tools my team built through LLM prompting, so QA review would not slow PR merges. One wrote tests in a separate PR and the other reviewed code. Both ran on real production PRs while we kept tuning them, and the review tool caught security issues on its first pass.
  • Rebuilt QA for the HuffPost apps, which had gone years without it, with manual and automated testing and a release process that tested before app store submission. Releases stopped needing rollbacks or pauses, and the rating rose from 2.20 to 3.75 in six months and now sits at 4.6.
  • Built a prompt-driven AI agent in VS Code that used the BigQuery CLI to answer usage-data questions in plain language. It removed my wait on the Data team and let me update device and OS coverage maps, in and out of scope, much faster. Shared them with product, engineering, and the CTO.
