I lead QA, and I build the tools.

I'm a QA manager at BuzzFeed and HuffPost. My team tests mobile apps on iOS and Android, and this year we've been moving from hand-checked releases to automated tests that AI helps write.

How I got here

I came up through QA and taught myself engineering along the way. I had never opened a terminal. Now I set up my own dev environment, work in Git, and take pull requests from first commit to merge.

The goal is to own the AI-powered QA infrastructure my team runs on, so routine changes don't wait in an engineering queue. This site is part of that: I designed it and built it with Claude.

How I work

  • QA decides what ships. AI can draft the tests. A person with risk judgment approves them.
  • Effort follows risk. Test generation limits are set by what a failure would cost.
  • One lane to release. A single delivery path keeps ownership clear.
  • Quality starts at planning. I work with product owners and engineering managers from requirements through release, not only at the testing step.
  • I fix the process, not just the bug. Release checklists, test management, and Jira epics came from tracing problems back to how work moved from idea to release.
